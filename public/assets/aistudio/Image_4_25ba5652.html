Chamber Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers regarding consultation sessions, retainer fees, conveyance timelines, and ethical compliance.

What is the standard consultation duration and fee structure?

expand_more
Standard initial chamber consultations run for 45 minutes of detailed one-on-one review with Advocate Deepak Goyal. Consultation fees are fixed, payable prior to chamber admission or commencement of tele-advisory, and are adjusted against full retainership should formal filing or deed drafting instructions follow.

How long does a comprehensive property title search report take?

expand_more
A standard 30-year non-encumbrance title search report across Sub-Registrar records and municipal bodies takes between 3 to 5 business working days. For urgent property registry transactions, expedited 48-hour vetting can be arranged upon initial review.

How is client confidentiality safeguarded during initial consultations?

expand_more
All discussions, whether in person or via telephonic channels, are strictly governed by professional legal privilege under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. Documents entrusted to the chamber remain encrypted and accessible solely to Advocate Deepak Goyal and designated chamber associates.

Can consultation occur at the client’s corporate office or residence?

expand_more
Special on-site consultations for senior citizens, high-net-worth estate executions, or institutional board briefings are accommodated on select Saturdays and Sundays by special prior appointment with the chamber clerk.
